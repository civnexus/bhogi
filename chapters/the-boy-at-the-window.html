Autobiography of a Bhogi · Fragment 03

The boy at the window

Music, mischief, and a world beyond the classroom.

Make yourself comfortable

In Tridib’s account of his childhood, piety and mischief share the same beginning. He remembers himself as a very naughty child, known for his mischievousness at school.

Music and sports held his interest. In the classroom, his attention wandered to the window: to birds and their sounds, to trees, to the sky. The world outside drew his eye while the teachers taught.

It is a small scene with which to open a life: a child at a window, listening to something beyond the lesson. The fuller recollections will follow; the birds are already here.

Opening fragment · An editorial adaptation of Tridib Nandy’s recollections, prepared for this first website concept. This is not yet a completed autobiographical chapter.
