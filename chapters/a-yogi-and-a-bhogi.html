Autobiography of a Bhogi · Fragment 01

A Yogi and a Bhogi

An inward path. A life turned toward the world.

Make yourself comfortable

Tridib Nandy begins with a familiar title: Autobiography of a Yogi. Then he changes a word. His story will be Autobiography of a Bhogi. In his telling, the bhogi is the indulgent man—the one drawn into the pleasures and experiences of life.

Yet his premise holds the two together. A life, he says, asks us to be both a yogi and a bhogi. The tension between them becomes the starting point of this book: the pull inward, and the invitation of the world outside.

These first recollections open a story still being told. They offer its central question: how might a life make room for both?

Opening fragment · An editorial adaptation of Tridib Nandy’s recollections, prepared for this first website concept. This is not yet a completed autobiographical chapter.
