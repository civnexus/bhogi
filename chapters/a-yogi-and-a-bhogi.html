Autobiography of a Bhogi · Chapter 3

The art of being both

A little stillness. A little appetite. A whole life.

Make yourself comfortable

There is a part of me that wants to sit quietly with the great questions. There is another that would like to know what is happening outside. They have been sharing a life for some time now.

The yogi and the bhogi make interesting companions. One listens for what lies beneath the noise; the other finds the music in it. One turns inward. The other opens a window. Between them is the possibility of a life large enough to contain more than a single answer.

I am interested in that meeting place. In the pleasure that makes us more attentive. In the pause that allows an experience to settle. In the moment when curiosity becomes a question worth carrying.

Balance sounds like a still thing until you try to live it. Then it becomes movement: leaning, noticing, returning. A little more patience here; a little more courage there. The work is in the adjustment.

These pages follow that movement. There will be room for delight and doubt, for mischief and reflection, for the ways a life escapes the neat description prepared for it. For now, the window is open.
