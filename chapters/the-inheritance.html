Autobiography of a Bhogi · Fragment 02

The inheritance

Ancestors who were yogis. A balance of his own.

Make yourself comfortable

Tridib describes his ancestors as yogis. He describes the family into which he was born as pious. These are the roots he names at the beginning of his story.

Against that background, he places himself with a mischievous sense of balance: in this life, he has come as a bhogi. His description carries both an awareness of what came before him and the promise of a life lived in his own way.

The family stories themselves are still to come. For now, this is the thread he gives us: yogi ancestors, and a descendant exploring the other side of the balance.

Opening fragment · An editorial adaptation of Tridib Nandy’s recollections, prepared for this first website concept. This is not yet a completed autobiographical chapter.
